# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to provide a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space for all
contributors and users.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive and assume good faith.
- Respect differing experiences and technical opinions.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, or publication of
private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates this
policy. Report concerns through the repository's private vulnerability
reporting flow when public discussion would expose personal information.
